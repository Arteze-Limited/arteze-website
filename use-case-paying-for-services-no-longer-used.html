Use case 01 · Manufacturing & Insurance

Paying for services no longer used

Retired services, unassigned seats and legacy fees that never stopped billing

The problem
Organisations change systems, restructure teams and move on, yet the invoices keep charging for retired services, unassigned seats and decommissioned circuits.
Why it happens
Accounts payable lacks the operational context to know what is still in use, and suppliers rarely volunteer to stop charging.
The commercial impact
Money spent every month with no business value in return, easy to miss because each line looks small on its own.
How Arteze helps
We reconcile live operational data against billing schedules line by line, then help you give notice on anything redundant.
What good looks likeA clear, costed list of services you could stop or reduce, with the notice periods and contract terms for each one.

What you gain

  • Lower running costs
  • Clean supplier billing
  • No operational risk
  • Budget freed for priorities

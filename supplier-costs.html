Supplier Cost Optimisation

How much are you paying for things you no longer need?

Supplier spend has a habit of creeping upwards when nobody is looking closely at usage, requirements or commitments. We pinpoint the costs that no longer earn their place, challenge the commercial arrangements behind them and help you get more from the contracts you already have.

Book a free conversation See how we help

Sound familiar?

The challenges we hear most often

  1. Duplicate, redundant or unassigned subscriptions and seats.
  2. Consumption reports that never get reconciled with invoice lines.
  3. Premium legacy rates for what is now commodity technology.
  4. No internal way to audit supplier billing accuracy.

How we help

What Arteze does about it

  • Spend and consumption reconciliation to stop leakage and surface rebates.
  • Independent challenge of supplier rate structures and catalogues.
  • Volume discounts negotiated and service capacity right-sized.
  • Continuous cost checkpoints that keep the savings in place.

Focus areas

Where we put our attention

  • Supplier spend analysis
  • Unused services
  • Duplicate services
  • Pricing analysis
  • Invoice reviews
  • Savings opportunities

The outcome

What changes for you

  • A clear view of which costs still earn their place.
  • Historical billing errors and credits recovered.
  • Supplier bills aligned with verifiable consumption.
  • No compromise on service reliability.

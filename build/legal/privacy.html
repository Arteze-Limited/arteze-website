What we collect

  • Details you send us by email or through the contact form: your name, work email, organisation, role, the topic you choose and your message.

How the contact form works

The contact form does not send data to a server. It opens your own email app with your details filled in, addressed to hello@arteze.co.uk. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app.

Why we use your data, and our lawful basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a call. Lawful basis: your consent and our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business, such as our email provider, under appropriate contracts. Where our delivery team outside the UK supports an engagement, we use appropriate safeguards for any international transfer.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for up to 24 months, unless we go on to work together, in which case our contract terms apply. We then delete them.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email hello@arteze.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk (opens in a new tab).

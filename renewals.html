Contract Negotiation & Renewal

Is your next renewal a risk, or your biggest opportunity?

A renewal is the moment to question everything: the terms, the requirements and the price. Arteze prepares the ground, shapes the negotiation strategy and sits beside you at the table until improved terms are agreed.

Book a free conversation See how we help

Sound familiar?

The challenges we hear most often

  1. Renewals entered late, with no leverage or credible alternatives.
  2. Restrictive lock-ins, punitive multi-year commitments and auto-renewals.
  3. No dedicated negotiation capacity or market pricing data in-house.
  4. Rigid change procedures that slow the business down.

How we help

What Arteze does about it

  • Renewal milestones set 6 to 12 months before notice deadlines.
  • Negotiation strategies backed by credible alternative sourcing options.
  • Renewal quotes challenged directly where pricing is out of line with the market.
  • Terms restructured for flexibility and fair price protection.

Focus areas

Where we put our attention

  • Renewal preparation
  • Proposal reviews
  • Commercial negotiation
  • Pricing challenges
  • Contract term reviews
  • Change management

The outcome

What changes for you

  • A better-prepared position on major technology and outsourcing renewals.
  • Protection against automatic indexation and unreasonable increases.
  • Balanced risk and stronger supplier commitments.
  • Clear, fair mechanisms for future change.

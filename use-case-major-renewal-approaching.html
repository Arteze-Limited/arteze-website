Use case 03 · Infrastructure & Financial Services

A major renewal is approaching

Building leverage, challenging price and avoiding lock-in

The problem
Renewal proposals often arrive late in the term, leaving little time to negotiate or explore alternatives.
Why it happens
Renewals compete with everyday work for attention, so they tend to start only when the supplier makes contact.
The commercial impact
Multi-year lock-ins, automatic indexation and uncompetitive pricing.
How Arteze helps
We open the renewal pipeline 6 to 12 months early, benchmark pricing, build credible alternatives and lead the negotiation.
What good looks likeIn an earlier role, our founder helped a major UK infrastructure programme bring a £7m software renewal quote down to £4m.

What you gain

  • Significant cost avoidance
  • Inflation caps
  • Operational agility
  • Balanced terms

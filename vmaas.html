Services / VMaaS

Vendor Management as a Service

What if every supplier invoice was checked, and every renewal was on your terms?

Most organisations verify only a fraction of their supplier invoices and learn about renewals when the supplier calls. VMaaS puts a dedicated Arteze team on the case: we check your supplier invoices against the contract, track every renewal milestone, recover the credits you are owed and run your supplier reviews, with a monthly ROI statement your finance team signs off.

Book a free conversation See how we help

Sound familiar?

The challenges we hear most often

  1. Invoices are approved because they look about right, and nobody has time to compare them line by line with the contract.
  2. Renewals arrive with too little runway to build real negotiating leverage.
  3. Earned SLA credits, volume rebates and indexation discounts sit unclaimed.
  4. Internal teams simply do not have the commercial bandwidth to govern suppliers systematically.

How we help

What Arteze does about it

  • Supplier invoices reconciled against contracted rate cards and delivery milestones.
  • Renewals tracked 6 to 12 months ahead, so negotiations start from strength.
  • Careful recovery of the credits and rebates your contracts already entitle you to.
  • A transparent monthly ROI statement, validated with your finance team.

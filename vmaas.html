01 What it covers

IT and multi-supplier governance.

Built for IT estates and multi-supplier (SIAM) models, where many suppliers have to work as one service.

Sourcing

Market scans, tenders and sourcing plans, run to a clear timetable.

Contracts

Contract registers, obligations, commercial review and change control.

Supplier performance

Scorecards, service reviews, credits claimed and issues tracked to closure.

Renewals

A renewal calendar that is watched, with negotiations started in good time.

Procurement admin

Requests, onboarding, purchase orders and records, handled to standard.

SIAM governance

Governance across multiple suppliers, so the service holds together end to end.

Enquire

Get more value from the suppliers you already pay.

Arteze helps organisations check, renegotiate and manage their IT and outsourced services contracts, so they pay the right price for what they actually use.

EnquireFind out more

Without regular checks, supplier costs tend to rise every year.

Prices go up, services that are no longer needed stay on the invoice, and credits you are owed go unclaimed. World Commerce & Contracting estimated in 2025 that poor contracting costs organisations close to 9% of their annual revenue.

Our experience covers more than 25 years and over £17m in supplier savings.

Our founder has managed suppliers and contracts for banks, government departments, national infrastructure programmes and insurers, always working on the client’s side.

£17m+in supplier costs saved, avoided or identified
25+years of experience managing large suppliers
£650m+of annual supplier spend brought under proper governance
80%less time taken to agree a contract change

These figures combine Arteze's client work with roles our founder held before setting up Arteze. The £17m is made up of around £10m in savings delivered, £6.7m in costs avoided and £0.7m in savings identified.

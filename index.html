Joint Account Plan

Two phases, one shared plan

We begin by establishing where the relationship genuinely stands. Then, if you choose to proceed, we help you and your client act on what we found.

1Phase 1

Maturity assessment

We find out how the relationship really stands, and what would make it stronger.

  • We agree the scope with you and choose who to speak to on both sides
  • We interview your client's people in confidence, so they can speak openly
  • We review the contract, service reports and account history through a buyer's eyes
  • You receive a clear view of the relationship's maturity, with our recommendations
2Phase 2

Putting it into practice

We help you act on the recommendations you have approved, together with your client.

  • We help you and your client agree a Joint Account Plan that both sides own
  • We support the quick wins and the longer-term changes
  • We set up simple ways to see whether the relationship is improving
  • You go into the next renewal knowing where you stand

It works as a health check on an account that is going well, or as a recovery plan for one under strain. We never advise a provider and its client on the same contract.

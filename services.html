Vendor Management as a Service

VMaaS

Most organisations verify only a fraction of their supplier invoices and learn about renewals when the supplier calls. VMaaS puts a dedicated Arteze team on the case: we check your supplier invoices against the contract, track every renewal milestone, recover the credits you are owed and run your supplier reviews, with a monthly ROI statement your finance team signs off.

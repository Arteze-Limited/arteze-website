The industries we work in

We focus on sectors where suppliers are central to how the organisation runs, and where contracts are large, complex or closely regulated.

Insurance

Insurers rely heavily on outsourcing and must meet the PRA's rules on how it is managed. We helped a UK insurer move from one provider to several, bring its contracts in line with those rules and save around £1m.

Banking and financial services

Banks depend on a small number of very large technology suppliers. Our founder built the supplier governance framework for a global bank's eight biggest IT suppliers, covering £550m of annual spend.

Government and the public sector

Public bodies run critical services through long, complex contracts that face close scrutiny. Our founder has led supplier strategy, governance and exit planning on contracts worth up to £100m a year.

Infrastructure and transport

Major programmes buy large amounts of software and IT services, often under time pressure. Our founder has challenged steep renewal quotes and settled licence disputes on a national infrastructure programme.

Energy and utilities

Regulated energy services often depend on a few critical technology suppliers. Our founder has managed the commercial relationships behind services of this kind.

Manufacturing

Manufacturers often run large IT estates that were bought for needs which have since changed. For a global manufacturer we identified around £700,000 of services that were no longer needed.

IT service providers

Managed service providers are seeing vendor prices rise while their resale margins shrink. We review vendor contracts and costs, and can run vendor management on their behalf.

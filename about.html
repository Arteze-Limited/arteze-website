About Arteze

Founder-led. UK-led.

Arteze Limited is a consultancy based in Milton Keynes. We help organisations find and keep the value they lose after contracts are signed.

01 Who we are

Experience, not theory.

Arteze is led by a founder with 25+ years in vendor management, supplier relationship management and complex contracting, including multi-supplier SIAM models.

We are UK-led. Our offshore delivery team in Hyderabad works inside client systems, under the client's own security controls. Your data stays with you.

We are independent. We take no supplier commissions and no resale margins, so our only interest is the value we find for you.

We support your team. We don't replace it.

About Arteze

Arteze is an independent consultancy based in Milton Keynes. We help organisations get the value they expected from their IT and outsourced services contracts.

Arteze was founded by a supplier management specialist with more than 25 years of experience. Over that time our founder has managed suppliers and contracts for a global bank, central government, a national infrastructure programme, large public-sector organisations and insurers, always working on the client’s side. You can read about the results of that work on our case studies page.

We are fully independent. We do not take commissions from suppliers or earn margins on anything we recommend, so our advice is based only on what is right for you.

Our leadership is in the UK and our delivery team is in Hyderabad, India. We work inside your systems and under your security controls, so your data stays with you. We work alongside your own team rather than replacing it, and your people keep the relationships and the decisions.

Our values are performance, value and innovation. We also built Tokvale, a tool that helps organisations charge Microsoft 365 and Copilot licence costs to the teams that use them.

Tell us about your suppliers and we will get back to you.

We reply to every enquiry within one business day.
